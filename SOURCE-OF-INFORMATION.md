# Source of Information — Punjab Jamabandi

**Last updated:** 20 November 2026

In the interest of transparency, this document explains **where the information in the app comes
from** and how it is obtained.

## Land records and administrative lists

- The **source of land‑record information is the official Punjab Government land‑records
  service** (the publicly accessible Punjab Land Records / Jamabandi portal).
- **District and tehsil data** is verified reference data maintained in the app.
- **Tehsils, villages and Jamabandi years** are fetched **live, on demand** from the official
  service's own public selection pages, one request per selection you make, and briefly cached.
  Nothing is fabricated and no large outdated dataset is bundled.
- **Records (Jamabandi / Fard / Mutation)** are retrieved from the official service only when
  you ask for them. The official record view is protected by a **CAPTCHA**:
  - The app shows the government CAPTCHA **inside the app** and **you solve it**.
  - The app **never bypasses, solves or circumvents** the CAPTCHA, authentication or any other
    security control.
  - The record returned by the official service (a PDF) is shown to you and is **not stored**.

## What the app never does

- It never **invents** owners, records, khasra / khewat / khatouni numbers, mutations or
  timestamps.
- It never **bypasses** CAPTCHA, authentication or government security.
- It never presents itself as an official government application.

## Farmer news

- Farmer and agriculture **news headlines** are gathered from **Google News** (a public RSS
  feed) for Punjab farming topics. Each headline links to the **original publisher**, where the
  full story is hosted under that publisher's own terms.

## Accuracy and verification

Information is provided for general guidance. Government data can change or be temporarily
unavailable, and the app has no control over the official service. **Always verify records
through the official Punjab Government service** before relying on them for any legal, financial
or property decision.
