# Privacy Policy — Punjab Jamabandi

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026
**App:** Punjab Jamabandi — Land Records & Jamabandi information utility (web / PWA / Android)

Punjab Jamabandi ("the app", "we", "us") is an **independent, privacy‑first utility** that helps
people in Punjab find and understand land‑record services — Jamabandi, Fard and Mutation
(Intqal) — along with a land calculator, a district directory, a glossary and farmer news.

> **The app is not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by the Government of Punjab or
> any of its departments.** Land‑record information is obtained from publicly available
> government services where permitted.

This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. In short: **you do not
need an account, and we do not collect or store your personal land‑record details on our
servers.**

---

## 1. Information we do NOT collect

- We do **not** require you to create an account or sign in.
- We do **not** ask for your name, phone number, address or any government ID.
- We do **not** store land‑owner names, khasra / khewat / khatouni numbers, or your search
  terms on our servers.
- We do **not** sell or rent any information to anyone.

## 2. Information processed to provide the service

To show you a land record, the app acts as a transparent relay to the **official Punjab
Government land‑records service**:

- You choose a district, tehsil, village, Jamabandi year and a khewat / khasra / khatouni
  number. These selections are sent **through our server to the official government service**
  solely to fetch the record you asked for.
- The official service protects every record with a **CAPTCHA**. The app **never bypasses,
  solves or circumvents that CAPTCHA** — it shows the government CAPTCHA inside the app and
  **you** type it. Your answer is forwarded to the official service to complete your request.
- The retrieved record (a PDF document) is passed to your screen and is **not stored** by us.
  The short‑lived session used to complete the request is kept only in server memory and is
  **automatically discarded after a short period (about 30 minutes) or when it is no longer
  needed**. It is never written to a database or shared.

We also provide **farmer news** headlines. These are fetched by our server from **Google News**
(a public RSS feed) for Punjab agriculture topics. No personal or device information is sent to
obtain the news.

## 3. Information stored only on your device

The following are stored **locally on your device** (browser storage / app storage) and are
**never sent to us**:

- Your **language** and **theme (light/dark)** preferences.
- An **install‑prompt** flag.
- Optional **recent searches** — these keep only the service and location you chose (district /
  tehsil / year), **never** the sensitive search value (owner name or khasra/khewat/khatouni
  number).
- **Notifications** you have seen — news headlines and their read/unread state.

You can clear recent searches and local data from **Settings**, and **uninstalling the app or
clearing site data removes all of it**.

## 4. Analytics

Analytics are **disabled by default**. If a privacy‑safe, self‑hosted analytics option is
enabled by the operator, only **non‑identifying events** (such as "search started" or
"calculator opened") may be recorded. Land‑owner names and search values are **never** sent to
analytics.

## 5. Advertising

Ads are **off by default**. If advertising is enabled, ads appear only on secondary,
information and tool pages — **never** on the main search action, form fields, record content or
source information. Ad providers may use their own identifiers subject to their own policies.
Ads never imitate government buttons, seals or notices.

## 6. Third‑party services and links

- **Official Punjab Government land‑records service** — used to retrieve records as described
  above, and where you complete any action that requires leaving the app. When you continue to
  that service, **its own terms and privacy practices apply**.
- **Google News** — used to fetch public farmer‑news headlines. Opening a story takes you to
  the original publisher's website, which has its own policies.

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third‑party sites you open from
the app.

## 7. How information is protected

- All network requests use secure transport (HTTPS).
- Any provider or source configuration is kept **server‑side only** and is never exposed to the
  browser.
- The app applies basic abuse protection (rate limiting) and returns sanitised error messages
  (no stack traces or internal details).

## 8. Children

The app is a general‑purpose utility and is **not directed at children**. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## 9. Your choices

- Use the app without an account.
- Clear recent searches and local data from **Settings**.
- Change language and theme at any time.
- Decline or dismiss in‑app notifications.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new
effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
